# Oxsa Nepal website image checklist

## First delivery: 26 original photos

These images will remove the main placeholders across the most important pages. Please send the original, highest-resolution files without text, frames, filters or screenshots. Landscape photos should ideally be at least 2000 px wide; animal portraits should be at least 1400 px on the shortest side.

### Shared across Home, About and Contact — 4 photos

1. **Oxsa's portrait** — a clear, warm photo of the German Shepherd who inspired the organization. Used on Home and About. Portrait or square.
2. **Current shelter exterior / wide view** — a welcoming, recent view of the shelter. Used on Home and Contact. Landscape, ideally 16:9.
3. **Founder or core team with animals** — natural group photo, not a formal passport-style pose. Used on About and Contact. Landscape.
4. **Early garage or shelter archive** — a photo from the early rescue years, especially around or after 2015. Used on About. Landscape.

### Our Work — 5 photos

1. **Spaying and neutering** — safe, respectful veterinary preparation or recovery; no graphic surgery.
2. **Adoption** — an Oxsa animal meeting or settling with an adoptive family.
3. **Daily feeding** — volunteers feeding community dogs or cats.
4. **Vaccination** — a veterinarian vaccinating an animal in a calm, humane setting.
5. **Awareness** — an Oxsa community session, school visit, outreach event or volunteer interaction.

All five should be landscape and visually distinct. The daily-feeding photo can also be reused on Donate.

## Delivery notes

- Send originals rather than social-media screenshots.
- Do not add text or logos over the photos; the website will handle cropping and overlays.
- Use filenames such as `puku-portrait.jpg`, `daily-feeding.jpg` and `shelter-exterior.jpg`.
- If a person is clearly recognizable, confirm that Oxsa Nepal has permission to publish the photo.
- Avoid graphic injury or surgery images; compassionate, dignified imagery will fit the site better.
